A shared time tracker for the Longform team. Know where every hour goes — and get sharper at estimating every project.

Get started

Three steps

STEP 01
Sign in
Click the magic link sent to your work email. No password needed.
STEP 02
Open your project
Your projects are listed on the home screen. Team projects show View →
STEP 03
Hit play & work
Start the section timer when you begin. Stop when you stop or switch.

Project ID

How to name your projects

Format
LF-### [Title]
Every project gets a Longform ID — e.g. LF-671. Use the same ID for your Frame.io link, Notion entry, and your exported file name so everything stays connected.

// Example: LF-671 Every Level of Claude Explained

Project Type

Sections vs Stages — pick what fits your workflow

▦Sections
Best for scripted content with multiple points
  • One card per video point (Hook, Point 1–16, Ending)
  • Every section gets the same task checklist automatically
  • Default tasks: Narrative Cut, B-Roll, Music & SFX, Text, Graphics, Roadmap, Mute swearing
  • Set the number of sections when creating the project
≡Stages
Best for tracking workflow by edit craft stage
  • One card per edit stage (Narrative Cut, B-Roll, Music & SFX…)
  • Each stage has a quality-control checklist from the edit playbook
  • Optional sub-tasks via + — add frame-specific or custom items
  • Time budget pre-assigned per stage based on 40h total

Features

What's inside

⏱

Timers & Time Budget

Every section or stage has a count-up timer and a countdown based on its share of the 40-hour total. The overall timer at the top sums everything across all sections.

Switch timers when you switch sections
✍️

Manual Time Entry

Forgot to start the timer? Use the ✎ button on any section to add or subtract time manually. The adjustment is logged in analytics and counted in your overall time.

🔁

Version Tracking (V1, V2…)

V1 is your main edit. Add versions for revisions — each has its own timer and checklist. Import Frame.io comments (CSV or TXT) to auto-generate a revision checklist from timecodes. The Overall Time filter lets you view V1 only, all versions, or a custom selection.

Default calendar day for V2+ is the 5th working day from project start
👥

Team View

Open any teammate's project to see their progress in real time. A VIEWING ONLY · READ-ONLY banner appears — you can browse but not edit.

📊

Analytics & Score

Every project gets a score out of 5.0 based on five criteria. Open it anytime from the ··· menu → Analytics. The Daily Activity chart shows tasks or hours per day in Bar or Line view. The Compare section shows your score against other projects and your historical average.

Going over budget on any section affects your score negatively
🔴

IDLE / ACTIVE Indicator

ACTIVE = a timer is running and syncing live. IDLE = no timer running. If sync looks stuck, refresh the page.

👁

Eye Care Reminders

Every 20 minutes of active timer time, a popup reminds you to step away. Timer pauses until you confirm. Toggle on/off from the ··· menu.

Section & Task Management

How to use the ··· menu

✓

Mark a Section Done

Hover over any section header — a checkmark icon appears on the left. Click it to check off all tasks in that section at once. Click again to uncheck them all.

✅

Mark All Done

··· → Mark All Done — checks off every task in every section and every version in one action. A confirmation appears first.

Cannot be undone in bulk
↕

Move Tasks

··· → Move Tasks — a move-mode bar appears at the top. Select a task, then choose which section to send it to.

⚙

Manage Sections

··· → Manage Sections — add, rename, reorder, or delete sections. Adding a section auto-redistributes the time budget. Roadmap is always fixed at 4h.

🕐

Manage Time

··· → Manage Time — adjust the time budget per section manually if the auto-distribution doesn't fit your workflow. Total stays within 40h.

Golden Rules

What makes the data useful

◆ non-negotiables
  • →Start your timer the moment you open a project. Stop it the moment you close or switch.
  • →Check off tasks as you complete them — not at the end of the day.
  • →One timer at a time. Switch sections → switch timers.
  • →Adding a revision? Import the Frame.io comments — don't rebuild the checklist manually.
  • →File name: LF-### [Title] — same ID everywhere.
  • →Time data is for learning, not judgment. Be honest with it.
